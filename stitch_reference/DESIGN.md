## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, civic-minded, and optimistic identity designed for an impactful urban non-profit organization. It unites high-trust institutional credibility with vibrant, warm community engagement. Built to balance grass-roots accessibility for newcomers and youth with rigorous professionalism for civic partners and institutional funders, the UI presents as elevated, luminous, and structured rather than sterile or bureaucratic.

### Visual Language & Aesthetic Direction
- **Modern Civic Editorial:** Influenced by contemporary cultural publications and progressive municipal platforms, the aesthetic employs generous negative space, refined typography, crisp architectural lines, and organic, humanizing contours.
- **Warmth Over Austerity:** Surfaces favor warm off-whites, soft creams, and light stones over cold digital greys, establishing an approachable, dignifying atmosphere.
- **Intentional Chromatic Restraint:** Color is deployed with precision. Lush green accents serve as directional beacons and symbols of growth and renewal, while a cardinal red micro-accent punctuates urgent milestones, badges, and civic alerts without skewing institutional sentiment toward tension.
- **Editorial Framing:** Layouts feature clear horizontal rules, subtle framing borders, structured badges, and curated content cards that elevate community narratives into stories of significant civic contribution.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate ratios to maintain a bright, publication-grade tone that never feels clinical, drab, or excessively branded.

### Palette Hierarchy & Ratios
- **Base Surfaces (55–60%):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Canvas) paired seamlessly with `#FAFAF7` (Warm Off-White) provides luminous grounding for body content and broad structural canvases.
- **Secondary Surfaces & Containers (15–20%):** `#F7F3EA` (Soft Cream), `#F1F2EF` (Soft Stone), and `#EDF5EF` (Pale Sage). These warm tier-two surfaces delineate modules, feature panels, side rails, and card backdrops without introducing heavy drop-shadows.
- **Typographic & Structural Weight (10–15%):** `#242424` (Charcoal Neutral) ensures AAA compliance against all tinted groundings for core readability, supported by `#66706B` (Slate Neutral) for secondary editorial commentary, metadata, and structural divider lines.
- **Primary Brand Accents (8–12%):** `#16A34A` (Vibrant Leaf) serves as the primary actionable focus, interactive state indicator, and forward progress anchor. `#15803D` (Deep Forest) acts as an anchor for focused text on green, deep state hovers, and high-contrast badges.
- **Micro Accent (2–4%):** `#D72638` (Cardinal Red) is restricted strictly to high-priority contextual tags, urgent community callouts, registration deadlines, and live status dots. It must never dominate section backgrounds.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Defined universally using `#E3E6E3` (Soft Warm Border), establishing delicate boundaries that remain gentle against cream and off-white backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic system is built on Plus Jakarta Sans, using its geometric geometry, friendly terminals, and crisp structural weights to articulate both personal warmth and institutional strength.

### Typographic Application Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and confident weights (Bold to ExtraBold). Headlines lead with editorial assertiveness, celebrating community triumphs and structural impact metrics.
- **The Eyebrow Convention:** Section headers and status markers frequently feature the `eyebrow-bold` style set in all-caps with generous character spacing (`0.08em`). This acts as an architectural guidepost above primary headings.
- **Body & Longform:** Standard body text prioritizes legibility with generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) in Charcoal `#242424`. Paragraph width must never exceed 68 characters to guarantee low cognitive load for multi-lingual readers, newcomers, and varied literacy audiences.
- **Secondary Details:** Timestamps, donor attributions, and administrative metadata leverage `body-sm` or `label-sm` rendered exclusively in `#66706B`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy balances rhythmic editorial structure with modular flexibility, utilizing an open 12-column grid system for large displays and a streamlined responsive stack for smaller devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `margin-desktop` (3rem) and `gutter-desktop` (2rem). Content cards and program indices span 3, 4, 6, or 8 columns. Maximum container width is constrained to `1280px` to maintain comfortable readability and balanced framing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin` (1.5rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Multi-column panels collapse predictably into 4-column balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Horizontal gutters compress to preserve edge padding, while complex programmatic tables transform into vertically stacked summary cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Internal component gaps prioritize compact relationships (`space-sm` to `space-md`), keeping titles, descriptions, and metadata visibly grouped.
- **Generous Canvas Separation:** Major thematic sections (e.g., transitioning from youth impact data to community intake forms) require `space-2xl` or `space-3xl` gaps. This breathing room creates an uncluttered, dignified presentation appropriate for institutional stakeholders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered surface tones and soft outlines rather than heavy digital dropshadows. This preserves a flat, editorial, ink-on-paper feeling while signaling touch targets cleanly.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat, un-elevated surfaces utilizing `#FAFAF7` or `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** Resting modules feature flat fills of `#FFFFFF`, `#F7F3EA`, or `#F1F2EF`, enclosed by a single 1px border of `#E3E6E3`. This provides definition without artificial volume.
- **Level 2 (Hovered / Floating Surfaces):** Interactive cards, menus, and popovers introduce a soft ambient tint: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.02)`. The ambient drop is warm-tinted and desaturated, avoiding stark grey blurs.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Drawers):** High-priority overlays layer on top of a soft `#242424` backdrop wash at 35% opacity with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`. The surface uses pure `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#E3E6E3` and deep ambient framing: `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -10px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces welcoming, generous contours that feel modern, approachable, and human. The rounded form language departs from sharp institutional angles, signaling warmth, openness, and safety for the community.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Rounding (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to compact interior controls: text inputs, standard checkboxes, dropdown menus, and internal status chips.
- **Large Rounding (1rem / 16px):** Standard application for buttons, floating action points, small modals, and secondary containers.
- **Extra-Large Rounding (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-2xl`):** The signature shape for core program cards, resource highlights, and testimonial panels.
- **Editorial Rounding (2rem / 32px - `rounded-3xl`):** Dedicated to high-level banner frames, highlighted thematic containers, and full-bleed hero wrappers.
- **Pill (9999px):** Applied exclusively to category filters, active status chips, micro-accent alert markers, and primary circular avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background of `#16A34A` with `#FFFFFF` text, `title-md` or `label-md` weight, fully rounded pill (`9999px`) or `1rem` contour. On hover, background shifts smoothly to `#15803D`. Internal padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#E3E6E3` border, text in `#242424`. Hover state shifts surface to `#F1F2EF` with a crisp `#242424` border.
- **Micro / Tertiary:** Subtle `#EDF5EF` pale sage background with `#15803D` typography. Perfect for compact internal interactions and low-friction catalog exploration.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact pills with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding. Rendered in `#EDF5EF` with `#15803D` typography for ongoing services.
- **Micro-Alerts & Urgency Tags:** Built with `#FDF2F2` (soft red wash), a fine `#FCA5A5` outline, and `#D72638` bold text (`eyebrow-bold` style). Used sparingly for open deadlines, grant alerts, or immediate community needs.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `#FFFFFF` surface and `#E3E6E3` border. Selected state swaps to `#242424` fill with `#FFFFFF` typography.

### Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height of 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border of 1px solid `#E3E6E3`, radius `0.5rem`, typography `body-md` in `#242424`. Focus shifts border to `#16A34A` accompanied by an accessible 3px focus ring in `#EDF5EF`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px boxes/circles with 1.5px border `#66706B`. Active checked state fills with `#16A34A` displaying a pure white check icon. Focus ring matches the input field specifications.

### Cards & Narrative Modules
- **Program & Resource Cards:** Built with `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) styling on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAF7` background with a 1px border in `#E3E6E3`. Cards feature generous internal padding (`space-lg`), high-contrast headlines, and an `eyebrow-bold` category badge pinned to the top left.
- **Impact & Metric Banners:** Soft stone (`#F1F2EF`) or warm cream (`#F7F3EA`) background wrapped in `rounded-3xl` geometry. Large numerals set in `display-xl` (`#242424`), accompanied by a `#16A34A` baseline indicator bar and small-print descriptive labels in `#66706B`.

### Community Story & Quote Highlights
- Editorial quote modules feature an asymmetric top border or decorative large-scale quotation mark in `#EDF5EF`, typography set in `headline-md` italicized Plus Jakarta Sans, and attribution badges resting on `#FAFAF7` surfaces.
